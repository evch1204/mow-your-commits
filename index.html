how it works

every day on your graph is a patch of grass. more commits, taller grass. drive over a day and the real tile pops out with the count. mow all 52 weeks and you get a time to brag about.

put your lawn in your README

a GitHub Action mows your graph every night and commits the picture to your profile repo. no tokens, no servers, nothing to host — three clicks and it is yours.
